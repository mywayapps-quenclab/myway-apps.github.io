GatherNext
Privacy Policy

Your calendars, shared only with your group

GatherNext is a shared calendar app that helps groups such as churches, sports leagues, and neighborhood associations create, join, and view shared events. This policy explains what information GatherNext collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address, used to sign you in.
  • The display name you choose, shown to other members of your calendars.

Calendar information

  • Calendars you create or join: calendar names, invite codes, your role (owner, admin, contributor, or member), and your membership.
  • Your settings for each calendar: color, whether it is shown, and notification preferences.
  • Events added to your calendars: title, date, time, location, notes, and how the event repeats.
  • Personal reminders you set on events.

Device information

  • A push notification token, a device identifier, the device platform (Android or iOS), and the device name, used to send notifications to your phone.

Crash and diagnostic information

  • If the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent that includes your device model, operating system version, app version, and technical details about the error. Crash reports are used only to find and fix problems.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

How we use information

  • Sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • Let you create and join calendars.
  • Show shared calendars and events to their members.
  • Send notifications about new and changed events, daily summaries, and your personal reminders.
  • Find and fix problems in the app.

Who can see your information

  • Members of a calendar can see that calendar's events and the display names of its members.
  • Your personal reminders are visible only to you.
  • Owners and admins of a calendar can see its members' email addresses, so they can manage who belongs to it.

Signing in

GatherNext does not use passwords. To sign in, you enter your email address and we email you a one-time sign-in link.

Service providers

GatherNext uses these service providers to operate the app. They process information only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — account sign-in, database storage, and backend services.
  • Resend — delivering sign-in emails.
  • Expo — delivering push notifications.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — delivering notifications to Android and iOS devices.
  • Sentry — crash and error reports.

No ads, no selling, no tracking

GatherNext contains no advertising. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. We share information only with the service providers listed above, or if required by law.

Data security

Information is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers, and access to calendar data is limited to that calendar's members. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as you have an account.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the menu and choose Delete account. Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account, email address, and display name.
  • Your calendar memberships, settings, and personal reminders.
  • Your registered devices.
  • Any calendar you are the only owner of, including all of its events, for every member.

Events you added to calendars owned by other people stay on those calendars, because they belong to that calendar's group.

You can also request deletion by emailing support@myway-apps.com.

Children's privacy

GatherNext is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date below will change accordingly.
