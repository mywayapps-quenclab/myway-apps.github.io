Ultimate Sticky Note
Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device

Ultimate Sticky Note does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Every list, task, and note you create is stored locally on your device only — nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions this app uses

  • Display over other apps — powers the floating note handle, the app's core feature.
  • Run as a foreground service / show notifications — keeps the floating note available and shows its status while active.
  • Start on device boot — restores the floating note after your device restarts, if you've granted the permission above.

Purchases

If you choose to unlock Ultimate Pro, that transaction is handled entirely by Google Play's own billing system. This app does not collect or store your payment information.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

This app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.
