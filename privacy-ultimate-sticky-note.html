Ultimate Sticky Note
Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device

Ultimate Sticky Note does not collect or transmit any personal information. Every list, task, and note you create is stored locally on your device only — nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions this app uses

  • Display over other apps — powers the floating note handle, the app's core feature.
  • Run as a foreground service — keeps the floating note available while it's turned on.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later) — shows the note's status and lets you cancel a snooze. The app works if you decline.
  • Start on device boot — restores the floating note after your device restarts, if you've granted the permission above.

The app may also ask you to turn off battery optimization so the floating note isn't closed by your phone. This only opens your phone's own settings screen; it gives the app no access to your data.

Backups

  • Backup files you create. Settings > Backup & Restore saves your lists and settings to a file in a location you choose. That file stays wherever you put it; it is never sent to the developer.
  • Android device backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's lists and settings in your Google account backup so they can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your Google account; the developer cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Purchases

If you choose to unlock Pro, that transaction is handled entirely by Google Play's own billing system, under Google's privacy policy. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that you own Pro, so it can unlock it. It never sees or stores your payment information.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

This app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It needs no account and works offline.

Deleting your data

All of your data lives on your device. Delete individual lists or items in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or using Settings > Apps > Ultimate Sticky Note > Storage > Clear data. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date below will change accordingly.
