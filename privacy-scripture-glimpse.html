Privacy Policy for Scripture Glimpse

Effective October 5, 2026

Overview

Scripture Glimpse does not collect, store, or share any personal information. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device and what happens when you open a passage in another service.

Scripture Glimpse is published by My Way Apps ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the Scripture Glimpse Android app.

Information we collect

We collect no information. Specifically, Scripture Glimpse:

  • Has no user accounts or sign-in
  • Contains no analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools
  • Shows no advertising
  • Does not access your contacts, location, photos, files, or other personal data
  • Does not send any data to us or to any server we operate

Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to sell, share, or disclose to anyone.

Settings stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences on your phone so it remembers your choices. These include your Old Testament / New Testament / All filter, whether daily notifications are on, the notification time, and the notification testament choice.

These settings never leave your device. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Notifications

Daily passage notifications are optional and off by default. If you turn them on, Android asks for your permission to show notifications.

Notifications are created and scheduled entirely on your device. No notification service, server, or third party is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in your phone's system settings.

Opening passages in Bible.com

When you tap a Scripture reference, Scripture Glimpse opens that passage in the YouVersion Bible app if it is installed, or on the Bible.com website if it is not. The only thing passed along is the passage reference and translation in the link.

YouVersion and Bible.com are operated by a third party, not by us. Once the passage opens, their own privacy policy applies, and we have no control over what information they collect. You can read it at bible.com/privacy.

Children

Scripture Glimpse is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version at this address and change the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting information, this policy will be updated before that change is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to My Way Apps at support@myway-apps.com.
